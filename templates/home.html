About Platform Architecture

How every assessment is scoped, executed, and mapped back to security guidance.

Automated VAPT Pipeline

Every scan runs a repeatable sequence — target validation, passive reconnaissance, header/cookie analysis, and structured reporting.

Boundary & SSRF Defense

Target URLs are resolved and validated before a request goes out. Loopback addresses, private IP ranges, and internal hostnames are blocked by default.

OWASP & Defense-in-Depth

Checks map to OWASP secure-headers and session-management guidance — CSP, HSTS, cookie flags, and HTTP verb enumeration.
